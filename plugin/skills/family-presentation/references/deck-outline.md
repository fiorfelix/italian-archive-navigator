# Deck outline

Adapt to the material; about 10–15 slides.

1. **Title:** the family or person, places, period. One strong image (a record crop or a
   photo from the archive).
2. **Where they came from:** the places, with their names and provinces at the time
   (`resolve_comune_history`), on a simple map or list.
3. **Timeline:** key events across generations, with years.
4. **One slide per generation or key person:** name and dates, two or three lines of story from
   the biography, the record image with caption (archive, series, year, act number).
5. **Records that tell the story:** a few notable documents (a marriage with its banns, a
   military service record), each captioned and cited.
6. **Still unknown:** open questions, and the records or offices that may answer them.
7. **Sources:** the archives and records used, with evidence labels explained in one line.

Style:

- Few words per slide; the story in the speaker notes.
- Captions under every image: what it is and where it comes from.
- A consistent, simple colour scheme; record images large enough to read.
- No details of living people.
