# Biography guide

A biography tells a person's life from the records, readably, without claiming more than the
records show.

## Structure

```markdown
# Giuseppe Rossi (1880–1952)

Giuseppe Antonio Rossi was born on 5 March 1880 in Montefiore, son of Luigi, a farmer, and
Maria Bianchi.[^1] In 1905 he married Rosa Verdi there.[^2] …

## Open questions

- Where did the family live before 1880? Next: the 1871–1881 population register (Comune).

[^1]: Comune di Montefiore, Nati 1880, atto n. 45 (VERIFIED).

[^2]: Comune di Montefiore, Matrimoni 1905, index (INDEX: act not yet seen).
```

## Writing rules

- Every statement has a footnote with its citation and evidence label.
- Say how certain things are: "the records show", "probably" (INFERENCE), "according to the
  family" (FAMILY_ACCOUNT). Never present an inference as a fact.
- Place events in context only when it's factual and relevant (for example the comune's name
  or province at the time, from the connector's `resolve_comune_history`).
- Keep it short: a few paragraphs, then open questions with suggested next records.
- Living people: not described; mention only that there are living descendants if relevant.
