Italian military service records: where to find them

Italian men were registered for military service by comune and year of birth. Their conscription lists and service records can tell you a lot, if you know which office to ask.

What exists

  • Conscription lists (liste di leva): every young man of a comune, listed by birth year (classe).
  • Service registers and records (ruoli matricolari and fogli matricolari): one entry per man, with his description, postings, campaigns and discharge.

Who holds them (Army)

  • Older birth classes: the State Archive (Archivio di Stato) of the province. By law, conscription lists go to the State Archive seventy years after the birth year of the class; which classes of service registers an archive holds varies by archive.
  • Later classes (enlisted men, NCOs and reserve officers): the Army documentation office (Ufficio documentale) responsible for the man's place of birth.
  • Career officers: from the 10th year after death (20th for generals), the Ministry of Defence (Direzione generale per il personale militare, 6th Division); before that, the documentation office for the place of residence.
  • If those fail: the Comune may still have its own copy of the conscription lists.

The Ministry of Defence publishes a table (Guida matricolare) of which office handles which requests; the request goes directly to the office it names. This guide covers the Army.

What to include in a request

  • The man's full name, date and comune of birth, and his father's name if known.
  • His birth class (year of birth) and, if known, his service number or unit.
  • That you are asking for a copy of the foglio matricolare, and your relationship.
