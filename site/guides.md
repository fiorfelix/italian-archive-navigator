# Guides to Italian family-history records

Short, sourced guides to finding and requesting Italian birth, marriage, death and military records.

- [**How to request an Italian ancestor's birth record**](https://archives.fiorfelix.com/guides/request-italian-birth-record)
  Who to write to, what to include, and which kind of copy to ask for (certificate, extract or full copy) when requesting a birth, marriage or death record from Italy.
- [**Italian civil records: Comune or State Archive?**](https://archives.fiorfelix.com/guides/comune-or-state-archive)
  Why Italian birth, marriage and death records exist in two originals, which one the Comune keeps, which one is at the Archivio di Stato, and where to look before 1866.
- [**When your ancestor's Italian town changed its name**](https://archives.fiorfelix.com/guides/italian-town-name-changes)
  Italian comuni have been renamed, merged, split and moved between provinces since 1861. How to find today's comune and the right office for an old place name.
- [**Italian military service records: where to find them**](https://archives.fiorfelix.com/guides/italian-military-records)
  Where Italian Army conscription lists and service records (fogli matricolari) are kept: the State Archive, the Army documentation office, or the Ministry of Defence.
